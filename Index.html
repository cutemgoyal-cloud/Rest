About

SubliMotion in Ibiza, Spain, is widely regarded as the world's most expensive restaurant, offering a 20-course tasting menu priced at $2,380 per person, according to Essential Home. This two-Michelin-starred establishment is known for its innovative and immersive dining experience, pushing the boundaries of gastronomy with cutting-edge technology and creative presentation. Another luxurious option is the Burj Al Arab in Dubai, a 7-star hotel known for its opulent atmosphere and high-end dining experiences, including a lavish breakfast buffet. Other contenders for the title of most luxurious include Aragawa in Tokyo, and various high-end restaurants in India like Avartana, The Table, Indian Accent, and Masque, which are celebrated for their innovative cuisine and luxurious settings.
